StudyKro — AI study tools for students

StudyKro is a free study platform that turns your own notes, textbooks and PDFs into practice material you can actually learn from. Paste a chapter, upload a lecture handout, or type a topic, and StudyKro helps you understand it, remember it, and test yourself on it.

What StudyKro does

The platform is built around techniques that cognitive science research has shown to work: active recall, spaced repetition, the Feynman technique, and structured revision planning. Instead of re-reading highlighted notes for hours, you use StudyKro to convert those notes into flashcards, quizzes, plain-language explanations, and revision schedules — the kinds of study material that actually move information into long-term memory.

The tools

  • AI Tutor — ask questions about material you're studying and get answers that cite back to your notes, so you can check the source instead of trusting a black box.
  • Flashcard generator — turn a topic or a document into question-and-answer cards designed for active recall practice.
  • Quiz generator — build multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes from your material to test recall before an exam.
  • Study plan builder — enter your exam date, subjects and daily free hours to get a realistic day-by-day revision schedule.
  • Concept explainer — get plain-English explanations, analogies and worked examples for topics you find confusing.
  • Essay outline tool — draft a thesis, supporting paragraphs and a conclusion structure for academic writing.
  • Mnemonic generator — create acronyms, memorable phrases and memory-palace prompts for lists you need to memorise.
  • Plagiarism checker — scan an essay for potentially unoriginal or AI-sounding passages before you submit it.
  • Exam tips — get subject-specific revision priorities and last-week preparation guidance.

Who it's for

StudyKro is aimed at high-school, college and university students preparing for coursework, quizzes and final exams. It's also used by self-taught learners working through textbooks, online courses or certification material on their own.

How to use it

Open any tool from the navigation, paste your notes or a topic, and generate your first output. Tools work in the browser — nothing to install and no account required to try them. If you want to save results, copy them into your own notes app; StudyKro doesn't lock your material behind a login or a paywall.

About the team

Free AI Study Tools — Flashcards, Quizzes & Summaries From Your Own Notes

StudyKro is a free AI study assistant for students. Paste your notes, upload a PDF, or type a topic and get flashcards, a quiz, a summary, or a full study plan in seconds. No sign-up, no credit card, no daily limits — every tool is free forever.

What can I do with StudyKro?

StudyKro takes the material you already have — lecture slides, a textbook chapter, class notes, or a PDF handout — and converts it into the kind of practice material that actually helps you remember: active-recall flashcards, self-test quizzes, plain-English explanations, and day-by-day revision plans. Everything is built around techniques cognitive-science research supports: active recall, spaced repetition, and the Feynman technique.

The free AI study tools

  • AI Tutor — ask questions about your material and get answers that cite back to your notes.
  • AI Flashcard Generator — turn a topic or PDF into question-and-answer cards for active recall.
  • AI Quiz Generator — build multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes to self-test before an exam.
  • Study Plan Builder — enter your exam date, subjects and free hours to get a realistic revision schedule.
  • Concept Explainer — plain-English explanations, analogies and worked examples for confusing topics.
  • Essay Outline Tool — thesis, supporting paragraphs and conclusion structure for academic writing.
  • Mnemonic Generator — acronyms, phrases and memory-palace prompts for lists you need to memorise.
  • Plagiarism Checker — scan essays for unoriginal or AI-sounding passages before submitting.
  • Exam Tips — subject-specific revision priorities and last-week prep guidance.

How do I use StudyKro?

  1. Open any tool from the navigation — no account needed.
  2. Paste your notes or upload a PDF (or just type a topic).
  3. Click Generate and get flashcards, a quiz, a summary or a plan in seconds.
  4. Self-test with active recall — the fastest way to move information into long-term memory.
